01 · Information we handle

The current app does not require a Fanumi account. You do not need to provide a phone number, identification number, or social media login to use local recordkeeping features.

Archives, timelines, and collections

Information: Names, birthdays or anniversary dates, titles, stories, tags, imported photos and videos, crop positions, frames, and themes that you choose to add.

Purpose and processing: Saving, finding, displaying, editing, and creating your selected cards or records on your device. The app reads properties such as media dimensions, type, and duration to display media correctly. These records and media are processed locally and are not uploaded to any server, except for an image you actively select for Alibaba Cloud cutout as described in section 3.

Reminders, rooms, and characters

Information: Reminder dates and text, character and room settings, furniture positions, interaction progress, and Star Coin records.

Purpose and processing: Keeping preferences and progress on your device and scheduling reminders through local system notifications. These records are not uploaded to any server. The current version has no account-based cloud sync.

Email you choose to send outside the app

Information: Your sending email address, description of the issue, and screenshots or attachments you choose to include.

Purpose and processing: We receive this information only when you actively contact us by email, to answer questions, handle feedback, and address privacy requests. The app does not send it automatically. Please do not send passwords, secret keys, identification documents, or unrelated private information.

The current app has no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not obtain your collections for advertising tracking or profiling, or sell your personal information.

02 · Optional permissions

  • Reading Photos: To select and import images or videos. Where iOS supports limited access, you may authorize only selected photos. Declining permission prevents the corresponding import feature from accessing your library; saved content remains available.
  • Adding to Photos: To save an image or card when you ask to do so. Declining prevents saving in this way, without preventing editing inside the app.
  • Notifications: For birthdays, anniversaries, and reminders you set. Disabling notifications stops system alerts; you can still view the records in the app.

Manage permissions in iPhone Settings under Fanumi or the relevant permission category; the exact location varies by iOS version. Current features do not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or cross-app advertising tracking permission.

Revoking Photos access does not delete copies you previously imported into the app. See section 6 for deletion options.

03 · Cloud cutout and portrait effects

Cloud image cutout is an optional online feature. The app explains it on first use and lets you choose whether to continue. Later processing occurs only when you actively request cutout for a selected image. The app does not scan and upload your whole gallery in the background.

When online processing is needed, the app uploads the selected image, potentially cropped and compressed for processing, to the Alibaba Cloud object storage used for this feature, then calls its human-body segmentation service. The service separates the subject from the background and returns a transparent-background image to your device. The request also involves technical information necessary for transmission, such as your IP address, request time, and request identifier.

This is image editing, not identity verification, face matching, or the creation of a face identity database. We do not use submitted images to train our models, for advertising, or for public display. Images may contain your portrait or other people's personal information. Submit only content you have the right to process, and avoid unnecessary sensitive content such as identification documents or private scenes.

Outline, glow, and relief effects are processed and rendered on your device. These effects do not upload images or effect data to servers. A relief effect is a visual representation, not a measurement of a person's actual three-dimensional body or identity.

Cutout results and necessary caches are saved on your device. Section 5 explains cloud source-image deletion and possible failures. Declining cloud cutout does not prevent basic local archives, timelines, or collections.

05 · Storage, retention, and security

Records on your device

Except for actively submitted cutout images, your archives, timelines, media copies, room settings, and other user content stay on your device. They remain there for your use and can be managed as described in section 6. The current version does not enable in-app iCloud sync. Whole-device backups, device transfers, or external exports you choose to use are governed by the relevant system or service.

The device may keep thumbnails, cutout results, and derived relief caches to avoid repeated processing. Caches are separate from originals or edited results you deliberately save. Deleting the app removes data in its app container; system backups and exported copies must be managed separately.

Temporary cutout files

Images uploaded to the Alibaba Cloud storage used for cutout have private access. Temporary access credentials are created for processing. After success or a failed request, the app sends a request to delete the source image.

A deletion request may fail because of lost connectivity, app interruption, or a cloud service error. An expired access link does not itself mean the file has been deleted. To check or request removal of a temporary image, contact us with the approximate processing time and a description of the issue. You do not need to resend the original image first.

The returned image is downloaded for local editing. Alibaba Cloud's own technical logs and handling of processing results follow its applicable service and privacy terms. You can contact us about related personal information requests.

Feedback and safeguards

Information you email is retained only as necessary to address the feedback, resolve related disputes, and meet legal obligations, and is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed and no legal retention duty applies. Contacting support does not add you to a marketing list.

We use safeguards such as encrypted network transport and private file access. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will take remedial steps and provide any notifications or reports required by law. Please also protect your device and keep the app and operating system updated.

06 · Your data and choices

  • Access, correction, and copies: View and edit content in the relevant archive, timeline, gallery, or collection. Use available save or share features for supported media exports. Other requests can be sent by email.
  • Delete an item: Use the relevant record's edit or delete action.
  • Clear archive data: Use “Clear local data” in settings to remove archives, timelines, related media, and corresponding reminders. Room preferences and some caches may be stored separately. To remove all data in the app container, use iOS “Delete App,” rather than “Offload App,” which keeps app data.
  • Manage copies: These actions do not delete originals in Photos, content already shared with others, or system backups. Manage those copies separately.
  • Withdraw permission: Turn off Photos or notification access in system settings. Stop requesting cloud cutout to stop further image uploads. Contact us to withdraw other consent or request deletion of information already processed.
  • Stop using Fanumi: The current version has no Fanumi account and therefore no separate account-deletion step. You can remove your data and stop using or delete the app.

Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing carried out before consent was withdrawn. We verify identity and relevant facts only as necessary for a request, without requiring unrelated information. We will respond promptly and explain any legal reason for not fulfilling a request and available next steps. You may also complain to the relevant privacy authority.

07 · Minors and local records

The same local storage rules apply to minors. Except for images a user actively submits for Alibaba Cloud cutout, the app does not upload minors' archives, photos, text, birthdays, or usage records to any server, or create server-side profiles of minors. The developer cannot remotely view, modify, or delete these local records. A guardian can help a minor view, edit, or delete them on the device as described in section 6.

Minors should read this policy and use the relevant features with a guardian's guidance. Cloud cutout uploads the image selected by the user. Where this involves the personal information of a child under 14, legally required consent from a parent or other guardian must be obtained first. Do not submit such images without the necessary authorization. Declining cutout does not prevent basic local collection features.

If a guardian believes an image was submitted to Alibaba Cloud without the necessary consent, they can email us to request investigation, cessation of processing, or deletion. This concerns only an image actually submitted to the cloud and contact information voluntarily provided. It does not mean the developer holds or can access other records on the device.

08 · Visiting these web pages

These pages require no login and have no image-upload or personal-information form. The page code does not set advertising trackers or behavioral analytics cookies.

These legal information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub. Technical information needed to deliver the pages may be processed outside mainland China; this is separate from the app's Alibaba Cloud cutout service in Shanghai. For processing details, contacts, and privacy rights, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection information. These pages do not receive your app collections, process your images online, or create user profiles.

Loading a web page sends technical request information such as an IP address, browser request information, path, and time to the server delivering it for transmission and necessary security. Visiting this page does not upload your Fanumi collections. External links and email services process related information under their own rules when you choose to use them.
